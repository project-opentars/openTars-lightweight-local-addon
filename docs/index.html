Tell your Linux desktop what to do.

openTARS is an AI assistant that does the work on your computer. It opens programs, presses buttons by their name, types, runs commands and looks at the screen. The AI runs on your own machine.

Ask the way you would ask a person.

In English or Portuguese, typed, spoken or from the terminal. A few things people ask, and what openTARS does with them:

  1. Open the calculator and work out 7 + 2

    Opens Calculator and presses each key by the text on it, through Linux accessibility. It works in milliseconds, even on Wayland.

  2. Make a folder called Invoices in my Documents

    Knows where your personal folders are, in your language, and creates it.

  3. What is using all my memory?

    Runs the command, reads the output and answers in plain words.

  4. Delete the old logs in Downloads

    Stops first. It shows you the exact command with Allow and Deny, and nothing is removed until you choose.

  5. Every weekday at 8, open the news

    Saves a routine that runs while openTARS is open, and tells you if one was skipped.

Nothing risky happens without you.

When a command could delete data or change the system, openTARS stops and waits for your answer. Stopping a reply also denies whatever was waiting. These always ask first:

  • rm -r
  • rm *
  • find -exec rm
  • curl | sh
  • sudo
  • ssh
  • dd
  • mkfs
  • git reset --hard
  • shutdown

The cloud is off unless you turn it on. If you do, your key stays on this computer and the window warns you whenever your text goes to a provider.

Two models made for it.

Both are downloaded by the installer through Ollama. You can still use any other Ollama model next to them.

Gargantua

The official AI. A Qwen3 4B model trained on thousands of real openTARS requests, each one checked, so it already knows every tool openTARS gives it.

Size
about 2.5 GB
Runs on
a graphics card with ~3 GB free, or the CPU with 8 GB of RAM

Cooper

An optional vision model that only looks at the screen. It finds the button you meant in apps that do not expose their controls to accessibility.

Install in one command.

It installs only what is missing: system packages, Ollama, its own Python environment and Gargantua. Updating keeps your chats and settings.

  1. Paste this in a terminal

    wget -O /tmp/opentars.deb https://github.com/project-opentars/openTars-lightweight-local-addon/raw/main/opentars_all.deb && sudo apt install -y --reinstall /tmp/opentars.deb

  2. Open openTARS from your app menu

    The first run picks your language, checks Ollama and downloads Gargantua with a progress bar you can pause.

  3. Run the self-test once

    opentars --autoteste checks your setup and asks the AI to open the calculator and do 7 + 2.

New versions are noticed by the app itself and installed after you confirm.
